The Journey

From Madinah
to Malaysia

Alburaq is named for the winged steed of the Night Journey — a fitting emblem for what we do: carry the finest of the Arabian harvest, swiftly and faithfully, across the world to your home.

  • ١

    Chosen at the source

    We buy direct from date groves in Madinah and honey valleys in Hadhramaut — no middle shelves, no guesswork.

  • ٢

    Handled with care

    Kept cool and clean from farm to freight, so texture and aroma arrive the way they left.

  • ٣

    Delivered to your door

    Cleared, stocked at our Seri Kembangan branch, and sent across Malaysia — or collect in person.
