Our Story

The steed that carries
the harvest home

In the traditions of the Night Journey, Al-Buraq was the luminous steed that carried the Prophet ﷺ across the heavens in a single night. We borrowed the name for a simple promise: to carry the very best of the Arabian harvest — swiftly, faithfully, and whole — from its source to your Malaysian table.

Since 2018

A licensed importer,
not a middleman

We founded Al Buraq International in Selangor to do one thing properly: bring genuine Arabian provisions into Malaysia without the guesswork. As a registered import company, we buy at the source, clear our own shipments, and stock everything at our branch in One South, Seri Kembangan — so what you order is what we can stand behind.

  • ◆

    Provenance we can name

    Ajwa and Mabroom from Madinah. Maraie and Sidr honey from Wadi Do'an. We tell you where it comes from because we know.

  • ◆

    Halal by default

    Every product is sourced and handled to halal standards — the baseline, not a badge.

  • ◆

    A real shop, real people

    Come to the branch, pay in person, or message us on WhatsApp. There's always someone who knows the stock.

What We Stand For

Three things we won't compromise

Honesty

If a harvest isn't good enough to serve our own family, it doesn't reach our shelves. We'd rather be out of stock than off the mark.

Freshness

Cool storage, careful handling, sensible quantities. Dates should be soft and honey should pour the way the valley intended.

Service

Fast replies, fair prices, and delivery across Malaysia. A small brand that answers when you knock.
